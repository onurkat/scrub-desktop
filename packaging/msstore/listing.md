# Scrub

Share the idea. Keep the details.

Scrub replaces sensitive details with reversible placeholders before you paste text into an AI chat. Copy an answer and restore the original details locally. Review each replacement, maintain your own dictionary, and process DOCX and text documents.

- Local processing, no uploads or telemetry
- Configurable clipboard shortcuts and review before copying
- Optional local mapping storage; clear saved mappings in Settings
- Free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later
